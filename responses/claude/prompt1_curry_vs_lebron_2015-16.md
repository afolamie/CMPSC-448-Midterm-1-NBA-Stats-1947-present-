# Prompt 1: Stephen Curry vs. LeBron James, 2015-16 regular season

Prompt: "Compare Stephen Curry and LeBron James during the 2015-16 NBA regular season using these statistics."

Both players logged almost identical workloads, so the comparison is fair on volume. Curry played 79 games and 2,700 minutes for Golden State; James played 76 games and 2,709 minutes for Cleveland. Their usage rates were also close (32.6% for Curry, 31.4% for James). The difference is what each player produced with that usage.

**Efficiency and overall impact.** Curry's numbers are the stronger of the two on almost every summary metric. His PER was 31.5 against 27.5 for James, and his true shooting percentage was .669 against .588, a gap of more than eight points. Win Shares tell the same story: Curry accumulated 17.9 (.318 per 48 minutes) versus 13.6 (.242 per 48) for James. Box Plus/Minus was 11.9 versus 9.0, and VORP was 9.5 versus 7.5. Curry's offensive edge drives most of this, with an OBPM of 10.3 against 7.0 and 13.8 offensive win shares against 9.6.

**Shot profile.** Curry took a very large share of his shots from three (3-point attempt rate .554, versus .199 for James). James relied more on getting to the line, with a free throw rate of .347 versus .250.

**Playmaking and rebounding.** James was the better distributor and rebounder in percentage terms: assist percentage of 36.0 versus 33.7, and total rebound percentage of 11.8 versus 8.6. He also led in block percentage (1.5 versus 0.4), while Curry led in steal percentage (3.0 versus 2.0). Turnover rates were nearly identical (12.9 for Curry, 13.2 for James).

**Defense.** The defensive metrics modestly favor James: DBPM of 2.0 versus 1.6, and 4.0 versus 4.1 defensive win shares, essentially a tie.

**Takeaway.** James was a more complete all-around contributor in rebounding, blocks and a slight playmaking edge, but Curry's combination of elite scoring efficiency, three-point volume and similar usage gave him the clearly higher overall value in this dataset, ahead on PER, TS%, WS/48, BPM and VORP.
